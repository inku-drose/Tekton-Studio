    FAQ

    Common questions

    How much does a website cost in Bengaluru?

    With Tekvara Studio, a landing page starts at ₹4,999, a portfolio website at ₹7,999, a business website at ₹14,999 and an online store at ₹29,999. You get a fixed written quote within 3 days of a free call.

    Are your websites SEO-friendly?

    Yes. Every site is fast, mobile-first and built with clear page titles, structured data and a sitemap. Search engine setup (₹2,999) adds Google Search Console and Google Business Profile, and basic setup is included in the Business website package.

    Do you work with clients outside Bengaluru?

    Yes. The studio is based in Bengaluru and works with clients across India. Calls, demos and handover can all happen online.

    How is the final price decided?

    After a free 30-minute call, we send a written quote within 3 days with the scope, timeline and a fixed price. The prices on this page are starting points for typical projects.

    Can my institute pay with a purchase order?

    Yes. We accept institutional purchase orders, UPI and bank transfer. Prices exclude applicable taxes.

    What do I need to provide?

    Your page text, logo, photos and product or record lists. Writing content, logo design, photography and entering large catalogues are not included in the prices.

    Will I own the website?

    Yes. Once paid in full, the website, its source code and all your data belong to you.

    How many rounds of changes do I get?

    Two rounds of changes at each stage. New features added after the scope is agreed are quoted separately.

    What happens after the free 30 days?

    You can pick a hosting or care plan, or run the site yourself. Work beyond a plan's included hours is ₹500 per hour.
